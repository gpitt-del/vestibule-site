Audiogram NOAH

Mild to moderate sloping sensorineural hearing loss bilaterally. SRT 35 and 40 dB HL. Word recognition 88% and 92%.

Tympanometry NOAH

Normal type 'A' tympanograms bilaterally with 1.1 and 1.2 mL ECV.

DPOAE · 12 frequencies NOAH

Right ear: present in the low frequencies, absent in the mid and high frequency bands. Left ear: present in the low frequencies, absent in the mid and high frequency bands.

Tinnitus

Tinnitus matched to a 6 kHz tone at 8 dB SL. Tinnitus was maskable at 12 dB SL and positive for partial residual inhibition.

Otoscopy

Unremarkable bilaterally.

Here's how a visit runs.

One patient, from the fax to the payment.

  1. The referral fax arrives.Vestibule reads it, makes the chart and texts the patient a link to book.
  2. The patient books.Reminders and confirmations go out by text on their own.
  3. They check in at the lobby kiosk.Patients check themselves in and sign their consent forms before you call them back.
  4. You test in Noah.The results land on the chart and the report writes itself.
  5. Two clicks.The report is signed and on its way to the referring physician.
  6. The claim is already drafted.One click sends it, and the payment posts when it comes back.
