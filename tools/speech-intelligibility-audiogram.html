Prototype for the counseling click-through. The speech canoe is the one from the audiometry printout; the hearing losses are invented. Drag any symbol to shape a different loss.

Voices and restaurant babble: CSTR VCTK Corpus (University of Edinburgh), CC BY 4.0, edited.

O right ear, X left ear. Everything above the line is outside that ear's hearing range; the gray letters are the speech sounds it misses. The index counts how much of conversational speech is within reach. The sound keeps playing when you switch between Normal hearing and Their hearing; only the filter changes. On headphones each ear gets its own filter. A voice you record or load is kept only in this page's memory and is gone when the page is reloaded.
