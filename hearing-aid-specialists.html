Here's how a visit runs.

One patient, from the fax to the payment.

  1. The referral fax arrives.Vestibule reads it, makes the chart and texts the patient a link to book.
  2. The patient books.Reminders and confirmations go out by text on their own.
  3. They check in at the lobby kiosk.Patients check themselves in and sign their consent forms before you call them back.
  4. You fit and verify in Noah.The real-ear match lands on the chart; counseling tools and the fitting report are a click away.
  5. Three clicks.The report is signed and on its way to the referring physician.
