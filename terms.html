Privacy

What this website collects

Only what you type into the demo form: your name, your practice's name, your email address and which demo you asked for. We use it to send your demo and to follow up with you about Vestibule. We do not sell it or pass it on for anyone else's marketing. To have it removed, write to help@vestibulehq.com.

Who helps us run the website

The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages), which keeps ordinary server logs such as IP address and browser type. The demo form sends your request to our inbox, either through your own email or through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, and our email runs on Google Workspace. The site's typeface loads from Google Fonts. The site sets no cookies and carries no advertising or analytics trackers.

Patient information in Vestibule

This website never asks for patient information. Inside the software, each practice's records belong to that practice. We handle them only to provide the service to that practice, under a Business Associate Agreement, and we do not sell them or use them for advertising. Records are encrypted in transit and at rest and hosted on Amazon Web Services, with access by role and a log of actions on patient records.

If you are a patient with a question about your own records, please contact your hearing care provider.

Data from Noah ES

When a practice connects its Noah ES account, Vestibule reads that practice's patient details and its test and fitting records, to keep its charts current and prepare its reports. That data is used only for that practice. It is not sold or shared with third parties, apart from disclosures the practice itself directs, such as a signed report to a referring physician. It is not used for advertising, profiling or training models, and it is not pooled or compared with other practices' data.

Vestibule keeps patient details in step between the two systems and never writes test results into Noah ES. Data that came from Noah ES is kept under the practice's own record-retention policy and deleted with that practice's records.
